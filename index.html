Roads at or below the water level are cut (bridges stay open). On foot you can wade through water up to 0.5 m deep (3× slower); deeper water blocks the road. Elevation is SRTM 30 m, so treat the result as indicative.

Fire spreads along the street graph (multi-source Dijkstra), faster downwind. Routes must reach every road node before the fire does (time-dependent search).

Each road segment fails at random; probability falls with distance from the epicentre and bridges are 15× more fragile. Fragility scales every probability. Red segments show one random outcome.
